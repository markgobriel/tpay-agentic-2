# Product evolution

The autonomous product team owns the whole in-scope product: strategy, information architecture, visual design, accessibility, interaction flows, functionality, content, tests, and removal of obsolete work. It may revise, replace, add, or delete any implementation, screen, flow, task, or idea when evidence indicates a clearer or more valuable in-scope outcome.

When no ready task exists, inspect the product as a novice user and replenish `backlog/ideas.json` with evidence-backed opportunities. Regularly audit existing work too: improve confusing flows, reduce clutter, strengthen accessibility, simplify overbuilt features, and retire work that no longer serves the product. Promote only ideas that strengthen clarity, planning, or routine follow-through without expanding into excluded product areas. Each promoted task needs explicit acceptance criteria and a validation plan.
