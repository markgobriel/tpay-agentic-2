# Product

Home Rhythm is an intentionally small, private home-routine planner for a single household. A person creates areas and recurring tasks, sees what is due, and records completion.

The MVP includes task creation, daily/weekly/monthly/custom-interval schedules, Today and Upcoming views, completion history, and pause/resume. It excludes accounts, integrations, notifications outside the device, professional repair advice, emergency guidance, health claims, and purchase recommendations.
